# A Pitfall Guide to Zizhu Third Cafeteria

Detailed address: Zizhu High-Tech Industrial Development Zone, Minhang District, Shanghai - Zizhu Third Cafeteria

I went to this cafeteria many times with my colleague jiuqi, and these are some notes we gathered from those meals.

## Pork Oil-Splashed Noodles (Wide Noodles)

<img src="./resources/images/pork-noddles.jpg" alt="pork_noddles" style="zoom:45%;" />

> [!note]
>
> Note that the wide noodles are hand-pulled on site by the chef. Personally, I think they taste better.
>
> The thin noodles, on the other hand, are not made on site. They are pre-made, and feel more like something bought from a market.

## Beef Oil-Splashed Noodles

jiuqi said the taste was decent, but compared with the price of 27 CNY, the pork oil-splashed noodles are still better value.

I tried some. The beef was all lean meat, so it was actually a bit dry. It would taste better with a little fat. The pork is also lean, but it is softer. Pork oil-splashed noodles are the GOAT.

<img src="./resources/images/beef-noddles.jpg" alt="beef_noddles" style="zoom:33%;" />

> [!note]
>
> Note that the peanuts are an extra add-on and cost 2 CNY.

## Saozi Noodles (Thin Noodles)

This includes Qishan saozi noodles and dry-mixed saozi noodles. After jiuqi and I tried them, neither of us recommends them.

## Roujiamo

jiuqi tried it and does not recommend it. The filling is chopped pork knuckle.

## Chicken Cutlet Rice

<img src="./resources/images/chicken-cutlet-rice.jpg" alt="rice" style="zoom:33%;" />

At the moment, I only recommend the chicken cutlet rice (18 CNY). It is affordable and tasty, but the good part is only the freshly fried chicken cutlet.

## Pork Tripe Chicken

I feel only the chicken tastes okay. Everything else is average. At 30 CNY, its value for money is even worse than the beef oil-splashed noodles.

The dry pot chicken wings actually taste pretty good too. I feel the chicken quality at this cafeteria is quite good overall.

# Dry Pot

Price list:

<img src="./resources/images/dry-pot-menu.jpeg" alt="menu" style="zoom:30%;" />

## Beef Dry Pot

The taste is average. How good it is depends on the side vegetables. Sometimes it comes with garlic sprouts, which I do not like. Sometimes it comes with potatoes, onions, green peppers, and red peppers, and then it tastes okay.

<img src="./resources/images/dry-pot-beef.jpg" alt="beef" style="zoom:50%;" />

## Chicken Wing Dry Pot

It tastes good. The chicken is very fresh. Most of the pieces are wing drumettes, so there are quite a lot of bones. It is also fairly salty.

<img src="./resources/images/dry-pot-chicken.jpeg" alt="chiken" style="zoom:25%;" />

Sometimes the side vegetables are rather plain, with only green peppers, but the taste is still the same.

<img src="./resources/images/dry-pot-chicken-green.jpg" alt="chicken-green pepper" style="zoom:50%;" />

## Bullfrog Dry Pot

I feel the taste is average and do not recommend it. The larger chunks of bullfrog meat do not absorb much flavor. Compared with the 28 CNY price, it is not really worth it.

<img src="./resources/images/frog-dry-pot.jpg" alt="frog" style="zoom:50%;" />

# Clay Pot Dishes

I feel they all taste very average, and they seem to all have glass noodles? I do not really like glass noodles.

## Lamb Clay Pot

The lamb clay pot has such a strong gamey lamb smell that it is basically impossible to eat. Definitely do not order it.

<img src="./resources/images/lamb-clay-pot.jpg" alt="lamp" style="zoom:30%;" />
